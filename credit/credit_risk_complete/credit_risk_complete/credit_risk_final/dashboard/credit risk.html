How to Read These Charts

Feature Importance bar
Longer bar = more influence. ext_score_mean alone explains 13% of model decisions.

Beeswarm (in Streamlit)
Each dot = one applicant. Red = high value. Left = reduces risk. Right = increases risk.

Waterfall (in Streamlit)
Shows each feature's push up/down from the 8% baseline for one applicant.

SHAP value
+0.12 means this feature increased default probability by 12 percentage points.
